# Research notes

This folder is intended for reproducible investigations and experiment records.

## Recommended structure

- `01_baseline_observation.md` — baseline summary and first findings
- `02_anomaly_validation.md` — validation of suspicious patterns against known indicators
- `03_detection_summary.md` — final notes and recommendations

## Template

Each research note should capture:

- research question
- dataset used
- method or algorithm
- metrics and thresholds
- assumptions
- result
- next steps
